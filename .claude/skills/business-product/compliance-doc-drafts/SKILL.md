# Skill: compliance-doc-drafts

## When To Activate

Use this skill when:
- the user needs a draft starting point for SaaS legal or compliance documents
- the task is to outline or draft Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie
  Policy, AUP, refund terms, DPA, SLA, or similar documents
- the user needs to know which legal pages are typically required before launch
- regulatory or customer requirements must be translated into a draft document

This skill produces draft starting points only. It does not replace legal
review.

## Context Prerequisites

- Check `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` first if it exists.
- Clarify the minimum business and legal context:
  - company name and entity
  - jurisdiction
  - product type
  - customer type
  - payment model
  - data collected
  - target geographies
- Clarify which document is needed first and why.

If the business serves regulated geographies or customer types, call that out
early instead of burying it in the draft.

## Discovery Questions

- What document or document set is needed?
- What personal or business data does the product collect, store, or process?
- Does the business serve EU, UK, or other regulated markets?
- What payment, cancellation, and refund model applies?
- Are there enterprise or B2B contracting needs such as DPA or SLA terms?
- What legal facts are still unknown and must be confirmed before a usable
  draft exists?

## Drafting Standards

- Every output must include an explicit “draft, not legal advice” notice.
- Keep placeholders obvious with `[CUSTOMIZE]` markers where business-specific
  legal facts are still missing.
- Match the draft to the document type instead of using one generic template for
  everything.
- Keep regulatory claims tied to known facts, not assumptions.
- Where relevant, surface the common required sections, such as:
  - ToS: acceptance, payments, IP, termination, liability, dispute resolution
  - Privacy Policy: data collected, use, sharing, retention, rights, transfers
  - Cookie Policy: cookie categories, inventory, consent, management
  - AUP: prohibited behavior and enforcement path
  - Refund/Cancellation: cancellation path, timing, data treatment, refund rules

## Output Structure

1. DRAFT NOTICE

Include the legal-review disclaimer at the top of every output.

2. CONTEXT SNAPSHOT

State the business, jurisdiction, product, and regulatory facts used for the
draft.

3. DOCUMENT DRAFT

Provide the requested draft with required sections and `[CUSTOMIZE]` markers
where needed.

4. CUSTOMIZATION POINTS

List the missing legal or business details that must be confirmed.

5. REVIEW CHECKLIST

State the attorney-review and compliance checks that should happen before use.

## Honesty And Non-Goals

- Do not present the draft as legal advice.
- Do not invent legal facts, jurisdictions, data practices, or compliance
  status.
- Do not imply that a generic draft is publication-ready.
- Do not hide missing business inputs that materially change the document.
- Do not promise regulatory sufficiency without qualified legal review.

## Required Notice

Every draft must include this notice:

> **This document is a draft starting point generated by AI. It is NOT legal advice. You MUST have a qualified attorney review and customize this document before publishing or relying on it. Legal requirements vary by jurisdiction, industry, and business model.**
