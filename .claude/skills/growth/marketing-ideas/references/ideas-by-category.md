# Marketing Ideas by Category

## Lead Generation

### Content Marketing
- Blog post series (10-15 posts)
- Case study collection (5-10)
- Whitepaper / ebook (3-5)
- Webinar / podcast appearances
- Guest posting campaigns
- SEO content calendar

### Low-Cost Ideas
- Newsletter growth ( referral program)
- Social media engagement (LinkedIn, Twitter)
- Community building (Discord, Slack)
- Email list building (lead magnets)
- PR / earned media
- Podcast interviews

### High-Effort Ideas
- Product launch campaign
- Account-based marketing (ABM)
- Influencer partnerships
- Event marketing (virtual/physical)
- Joint ventures / partnerships
- Referral program with incentives

## SaaS Specific

### Activation & Retention
- Onboarding email sequence
- Feature announcement campaign
- Usage-based email triggers
- Success check-in sequence
- Win-back campaign

### Expansion Revenue
- Upsell campaign (free → paid)

- Feature education series

## Metrics to Track

| Campaign Type | Key Metrics |
|--------------|-------------|
| Lead Gen | MQLs, Cost per MQL, Conversion rate |
| Content | Page views, Time on page, Shares |
| Email | Open rate, Click rate, Unsubscribe rate |
| Social | Engagement rate, Follower growth |
| Paid | ROAS, CPA, Conversion rate |
| Referral | Shares, Program participation |
