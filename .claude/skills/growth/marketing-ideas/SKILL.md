# Skill: marketing-ideas

## When To Activate

Use this skill when:
- the user needs strategic marketing ideas rather than execution of one channel
- the task is brainstorming growth options for a product with limited direction
- the user needs a shortlist of tactics matched to stage, budget, and team
- the goal is to identify the next promising marketing experiments

Use a channel-specific skill once the user commits to a concrete execution path.

## Context Prerequisites

- Check `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` first if it exists.
- Clarify:
  - product and audience
  - current stage
  - growth goal
  - budget and team size
  - what has already been tried
- Treat this skill as triage into the right next tactics, not as a replacement
  for deeper channel strategy.

Use the complete idea library in
[references/ideas-by-category.md](references/ideas-by-category.md) instead of
trying to surface every option from memory.

## Discovery Questions

- What is the main growth goal right now?
- What is the tightest constraint:
  - budget
  - time
  - team
  - audience access
- What channels have worked or failed so far?
- Does the team need:
  - fast wins
  - compounding growth
  - authority building
  - product-led acquisition
- What stage is the product in:
  - pre-launch
  - early
  - growth
  - scale

## Decision Framework

- Recommend only a small set of ideas that fit the actual context.
- Match ideas to stage, budget, and execution capacity.
- Distinguish quick wins from compounding bets.
- Prefer ideas with a natural follow-on skill path when execution gets deeper.
- Explain why each recommendation fits instead of dumping a long tactic list.

## Output Structure

1. CONTEXT SNAPSHOT

State the stage, goal, and key constraints driving the recommendation.

2. PRIORITIZED IDEAS

Provide the top 3-5 ideas, each with:
- what it is
- why it fits
- how to start
- what success would look like

3. RESOURCE CHECK

State the time, budget, and capability burden for the recommended ideas.

4. NEXT EXECUTION PATH

State which specialist skill or workstream should handle each chosen idea next.

## Honesty And Non-Goals

- Do not dump a long library of tactics when the user needs prioritization.
- Do not recommend channels with no plausible audience fit.
- Do not promise results from an idea without execution and iteration.
- Do not blur brainstorming and channel execution into one step.
- Do not ignore the user’s real operating constraints.
