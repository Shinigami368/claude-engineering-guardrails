# AI Search Platform Ranking Factors

## Perplexity

### Content Ranking Factors
| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Factual Accuracy** | Very High | Citations must be verifiable |
| **Recency** | High | Favors recent sources |
| **Domain Authority** | Medium | Trusted sources rank higher |
| **Structure** | Medium | Clear headings, bullet points preferred |
| **Comprehensiveness** | Medium | Complete answers over partial |
| **Source Diversity** | Medium | Multiple sources cited |

### What Triggers Citations
- Direct factual claims
- Statistics and numbers
- Definitional content
- Step-by-step instructions
- Current events information

### What NOT to Expect
- Product pages (usually commercial)
- Thin content
- First-person opinions without evidence
- Outdated information

## ChatGPT (with Browse)

### Content Factors
| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Authority** | High | Established publications, authors |
| **Clarity** | High | Well-structured, scannable |
| **Accuracy** | Critical | No hallucinations in training |
| **Freshness** | Medium | GPT-4 has knowledge cutoff |
| **User Engagement** | Medium | If people click/stay on your site |

### Scaled Ranking
OpenAI doesn't officially index for ChatGPT. However, content may be cited if:
- It's from a high-authority source
- It appears in training data (pre-2023 for GPT-4)
- It gets linked/referenced by cited sources

## Common Ranking Factors Across Platforms

### Always Important
1. **Accuracy** — Factual correctness is non-negotiable
2. **Authority** — Who wrote it? What's their expertise?
3. **Transparency** — Clear sourcing, no hidden agendas
4. **Structure** — Headings, bullets, numbered lists help parsing
5. **Completeness** — Answer the full question

### Content Characteristics

#### High-Performance Content
- FAQ-style headers matching user queries
- Statistics with citations
- Step-by-step instructions
- Definitions with examples
- Lists and comparison tables
- Recent data (within 6-12 months)

#### Low-Performance Content
- Thin / superficial coverage
- No structure (walls of text)
- Outdated statistics
- Self-promotional without value
- Opinion presented as fact

### Technical Factors

| Factor | AI Search Impact |
|--------|------------------|
| Schema Markup | Helps with rich snippets |
| Page Speed | Minor (user experience) |
| Mobile-Friendly | Minor (user experience) |
| HTTPS | Expected |
| Clear Navigation | Helps crawlers |

## Industry-Specific Notes

### Finance / Investing
- **Must**: Disclosures, date stamps, official sources
- **Authority**: Bloomberg, Reuters, SEC filings
- **Avoid**: Predictions without caveats

### Health / Medical
- **Must**: Medical professional authorship, citations
- **Authority**: Mayo Clinic, WebMD, peer-reviewed
- **Avoid**: Home remedies, unproven treatments

### Legal
- **Must**: Qualified attorney content, state-specific
- **Authority**: Caselaw, official regulations
- **Avoid**: "I am not a lawyer" disclaimers

### Technology
- **Freshness**: Critical (6 months max)
- **Authority**: Technical accuracy, official docs
- **Code/Tutorials**: Highly valued

### Product Reviews
- **Must**: Actual testing, pros/cons, comparisons
- **Authority**: Hands-on experience
- **Avoid**: Thin specs copied from manufacturer
