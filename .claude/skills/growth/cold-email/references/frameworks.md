# Cold Email Frameworks

## AIDA Framework

Attention → Interest → Desire → Action

### Example
```
Subject: Your competitor just closed Series B

Hi [Name],

[Attention] You might have seen that Acme Corp 
raised $50M last week.

[Interest] While they were building, 
their sales team was using a playbook 
we helped create that's now standard 
at 200+ B2B companies.

[Desire] The same approach could work for 
[Company] — especially as you scale past 
the [X] rep mark where most companies hit friction.

[Action] Want to see how it works in 20 minutes?
```

## PAS Framework

Problem → Agitation → Solution

### Example
```
Subject: The real reason your trial conversion is stuck at 3%

Hi [Name],

[Problem] Most PLG companies I've talked to 
struggle to convert trials beyond 3%, 
even with strong product-market fit.

[Agitation] That gap usually costs $500K+ 
in ARR by the time you hit 1,000 trials. 
Worse, it masks whether your product 
is actually sticky.

[Solution] We help teams identify exactly 
where trials are dropping off and 
build the right activation flows.

Can I show you what that looks like for 
companies at your stage?
```

## BAB Framework

Before → After → Bridge

### Example
```
Subject: What changed for the RevOps teams 
who hit 150% of quota

Hi [Name],

[Before] Six months ago, most RevOps leaders 
I talked to were spending 60% of their time 
on manual reporting and 20% on strategy.

[After] Now the ones hitting quota have 
cut reporting to 20% and use the other 40% 
on stuff that actually moves the needle.

[Bridge] We built a simple setup that lets 
teams do this in about two weeks without 
ripping out their current stack.

Worth a quick look?
```

## Question-Lead Framework

### Example
```
Subject: Quick question about [Company]

Hi [Name],

Have you looked at how [Company]'s Q4 
expansion is being tracked across the org?

We work with RevOps teams who thought 
they had visibility — until they 
realized there were [X] gaps in how 
expansion gets attributed.

Worth a 10-minute call to show you 
what a complete picture looks like?
```

## Story-Led Framework

### Example
```
Subject: What happened when [Similar Company] 
stopped guessing at growth

Hi [Name],

Last quarter, [Similar Company]'s RevOps team 
was exactly where you might be — trying to 
figure out why pipeline was healthy but 
conversion was dropping.

Three months after implementing a simple 
attribution model, they saw:
- 23% lift in win rate
- 40% reduction in deal stall time
- Clearer coaching convos with AEs

The secret wasn't a new tool. It was 
getting everyone on the same data story.

Same situation? I can share what they did.
```

## Contrast Framework

### Example
```
Subject: What 80th percentile SaaS companies 
do differently at scale

Hi [Name],

Companies in the top quartile for revenue growth 
don't necessarily spend more on sales and marketing.

But they almost always do one thing differently:

[Specific differentiator]

Most teams I've worked with didn't realize 
they were missing this until we mapped it out.

Same on your team?
```

## Framework Selection Guide

| Situation | Best Framework |
|-----------|----------------|
| Prospect seems skeptical | AIDA (builds gradually) |
| Prospect aware of problem | PAS (agitates pain) |
| Prospect needs hope | BAB (before/after) |
| Prospect is curious | Question-Lead |
| Prospect is analytical | Story-Led |
| Prospect needs proof | Contrast (data-driven) |

## Subject Line Formulas

### Question Subjects
```
Quick question about [Company]
Have you tried [X] for [Y]?
What's your take on [trend]?
```

### Number/List Subjects
```
[X] ways [Company] could improve [metric]
[X] things we learned from [Similar Company]
The [X] problem[s] most [role] face
```

### Curiosity Gap
```
Saw what you did at [Company]...
This isn't the email you expected...
You might be right, but...
```

### Name-Drop
```
[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out
I work with [similar company] on...
```

### Benefit-First
```
How to [achieve X] without [common pain]
[X]% lift in [metric] — here's how
```

## Body Length Guidelines

### Short Email (50-100 words)
```
Hi [Name],

[One-sentence observation or compliment]

[One-sentence your insight or value prop]

[One-sentence ask]

[One-click link or signature]
```

### Medium Email (100-200 words)
```
Hi [Name],

[Hook - 1-2 sentences]

[Problem/insight - 2-3 sentences]

[Solution/value - 3-4 sentences]

[Social proof or credibility - 1-2 sentences]

[Ask - 1-2 sentences]
```

### Long Email (200+ words)
Only for:
- C-suite with complex sales
- High-involvement purchases
- Executive sponsors needing full context

Generally: shorter = better for cold email.
