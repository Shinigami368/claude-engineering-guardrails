### Conversion Funnels
```
Cold Email Sent
     ↓
Opens (25-35% average)
     ↓
Replies (5-15% average)
     ↓
Meetings Booked (20-30% of replies)
     ↓
Qualified Leads (30-50% of meetings)
     ↓
Customers (10-20% of qualified)
```

## Expert Methods for Higher Response Rates

### 1. The "Video Prospecting" Method
- Include a Loom/Vidyard video (30-60 sec)
- Increases reply rate by 2-3x
- Best for: Gatekeepers, C-suite, warm prospects

### 2. The "Quiz/Assessment" Hook
- Offer a free assessment or audit
- Creates value upfront
- Best for: Marketing managers, agency prospects

### 3. The "Mutual Connection" Approach
- Name-drop shared network
- Increases credibility
- Best for: LinkedIn connections, warm introductions

### 4. The "Contrarian Claim" Hook
- Open with a surprising statistic or claim
- Forces curiosity open
- Best for: Founders, VPs, Directors

### 5. The "Paper Trail" Method
- Reference specific content (article, tweet, case study)
- Shows research was done
- Best for: Thought leaders, publicly vocal prospects

## Common Mistakes That Kill Response Rates

### ❌ Mistake 1: Vague Subject Lines
```
Bad:  "Quick question"
Better: "[Mutual Connection] + [Specific Topic]"
Best:  "The [X] mistake [Company] is making in [Area]"
```

### ❌ Mistake 2: Writing Novels
- Keep emails under 100 words
- One clear CTA
- Maximum 3 sentences per paragraph

### ❌ Mistake 3: Ignoring Email Preview Text
- Preview text = second chance at subject line
- Never leave blank
- Should complement not repeat subject

### ❌ Mistake 4: Wrong "From" Name
- Personal name > Company name as sender
- "John from Acme" not "Acme Marketing Team"
- First name increases open rates by 10-15%

### ❌ Mistake 5: Generic Value Propositions
- "Save time, make money" = NO
- "How [Specific Company] reduced [Specific Metric] by [X]%" = YES

## Optimal Send Times

### Best Days
1. Tuesday (highest response rate)
2. Wednesday (close second)
3. Thursday (morning only)
4. Monday (avoid afternoon)

### Best Times (Prospect's Timezone)
- 8-10 AM: First catch before meetings
- 11 AM-12 PM: Post-meeting inbox check
- 4-5 PM: End of day scan

### Worst Times
- Friday afternoon
- Monday morning
- Post-holiday days

## A/B Testing Recommendations

### What to Test
1. **Subject lines** (always test first)
2. **Email length** (short vs. medium)
3. **Personalization depth** (name only vs. full research)
4. **CTA type** (calendar link vs. reply)
5. **Send time**

### Minimum Sample Size
- 100 sends per variant minimum
- 2 weeks minimum test duration
- 95% confidence level before declaring winner
