# Cold Email Personalization Techniques

## Basic Personalization (High ROI)

### Name + Company
```markdown
Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company] is hiring [Role]...

---

Hi Sarah,

I saw HubSpot just raised their Series D...
```

### Industry + Pain Point
```markdown
I work with a lot of [industry] companies on [problem]...

---

I help B2B SaaS companies reduce churn...
```

### Job Title + Recent News
```markdown
As a [title], you're probably dealing with [challenge]...

---

VPs of Marketing are usually swamped with attribution reporting...
```

## Intermediate Personalization

### Mutual Connection
```markdown
We both know [Mutual Connection] — they mentioned 
I should reach out about [topic].

---

You and I both know Marcus Chen from the Salesforce 
partner summit. He said you might be looking at RevOps tools...
```

### LinkedIn Profile
```markdown
I noticed you've been active in the [topic] space...

---

Your recent post about demand gen was spot-on, especially 
the point about [specific insight]...
```

### Company-Specific
```markdown
Congrats on [Company]'s [recent news]...

---

Saw that Notion hit 1M users last month. 
The productivity space is getting crowded — happy to share 
what's working for other [similar companies]...
```

## Advanced Personalization

### Multi-Touch Research
```
Research layers:
1. Company website (what they do, who they serve)
2. LinkedIn (recent posts, job changes, connections)
3. Twitter/X (what they tweet about)
4. News (funding, launches, press)
5. Podcast appearances
6. Guest articles
7. Review sites (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot)
```

### Example: Deep Research Sequence
```markdown
Subject: Notion → Coda migration

Hi [First Name],

You recently joined the [Company] team as Head of RevOps. 
Before that you were at [Previous Company] building out 
the sales process from scratch.

I work with RevOps leaders who are navigating the 
[specific challenge you've likely faced based on background]...

[Specific insight relevant to their career path]

Would love to share what's working for others in your space.

---

Sarah,

Your G2 review of Coda mentioned the formula features 
are "finally good enough to replace Notion." 

That's been the key unlock for a lot of teams 
I've been working with...
```

## Personalization Formulas

### The "Observation + Insight + Value" Formula
```
Observation: I noticed [specific thing about them]
Insight: This usually means [what it implies]
Value: I help [audience] do [better outcome]
CTA: Want to hear what's working for others?
```

### The "Compliment + Question" Formula
```
Compliment: [Genuine specific compliment about their work]
Question: Have you considered [related challenge]?
Offer: I might be able to share [specific value]
```

### The "Photo / Screenshot" Formula
```
"I noticed your [profile/website/post]..."

Screenshot + annotation

"This made me think about [insight]..."
```

### The "Shared Interest" Formula
```
"I see you [interest/activity]..."

"[Related thing I noticed]..."

"[How this connects to what I do]"
```

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Fake Personalization
```markdown
"Hi {{first_name}}, I saw your company {{company}}..."

Never use obvious template tokens!
```

### ❌ Over-Personalization
- Don't mention family photos (creepy)
- Don't reference private LinkedIn info
- Don't overstep bounds

### ❌ Generic Insights
```markdown
"Companies need to reduce costs..."

Be specific:
"Series B SaaS companies with 50+ reps 
typically lose $200K/yr to inefficient onboarding..."
```
