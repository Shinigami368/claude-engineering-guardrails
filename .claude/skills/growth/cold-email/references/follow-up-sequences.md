# Follow-Up Sequences

## Why Follow-Up Matters

| Email # | Response Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| 1st email | 5-10% |
| 2nd email (follow-up) | +3-5% |
| 3rd email | +1-2% |
| 4th+ email | <1% |

Most prospects respond on email 2-3. Follow-up increases reply rate by 50%+.

---

## Follow-Up Timing

### Standard Sequence
```
Day 0: Initial email
Day 3-5: First follow-up
Day 7-10: Second follow-up
Day 14-21: Third follow-up (break-up email)
```

### Quick Sequence (fast-moving prospects)
```
Day 0: Initial
Day 2: First follow-up
Day 5: Second follow-up
Day 10: Final
```

### Long Sequence (enterprise/C-suite)
```
Day 0: Initial
Day 5: First follow-up
Day 12: Second follow-up
Day 21: Third follow-up
Day 35: Break-up email
```

## Follow-Up Templates

### First Follow-Up (Day 3-5)

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

Just wanted to bump this up in your inbox.

[One specific insight or question]

[Clear, low-friction CTA]

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Second Follow-Up (Day 7-10)

```
Subject: [Name] - still interested?

Hi [Name],

I know you're busy. I kept this short.

[New angle or piece of information]

Worth a 10-min call?

[Calendar link]
```

### Third Follow-Up / Break-Up Email (Day 14-21)

```
Subject: [Name] - one last thing

Hi [Name],

I'll stop bothering you after this.

[One final insight or value prop]

If it's not relevant, I get it. But if it could help with [pain point], 
I'm here.

[Link to resource or calendar]

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Break-Up Email Variant

```
Subject: [Name], I'll leave you alone

Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times without hearing back. Clearly now's not the time.

I'm going to close your file for now. But if things change or you 
have questions later, feel free to reply.

Good luck with [their goal].

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Best Practices

### DO
- ✅ Reference previous email briefly
- ✅ Add new value in each follow-up
- ✅ Change subject line (remove "Re:" and add new angle)
- ✅ Use different formats (text-only, short paragraph, bullet points)
- ✅ Include clear, easy CTA
- ✅ Stop after 3-4 emails

### DON'T
- ❌ Send same email again
- ❌ Be guilt-tripping or aggressive
- ❌ Use read receipts/tracking without disclosure
- ❌ Follow up more than 4 times
- ❌ Send same subject line

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Automated "Did you see my last email?"
This feels impersonal. Reference something specific.

### ❌ Changing Nothing
Same subject, same format, same day = ignored.

### ❌ Too Frequent
<3 days between emails = spam/unprofessional.

### ❌ Too Many Emails
5+ emails = unsubscribe + negative brand association.
