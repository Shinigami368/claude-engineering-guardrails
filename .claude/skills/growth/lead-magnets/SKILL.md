# Skill: lead-magnets

## When To Activate

Use this skill when:
- the user wants a downloadable or gated offer for lead capture
- the task is to choose the right lead magnet format, topic, or gating model
- the user needs a landing-page, distribution, or measurement plan for a lead
  magnet
- the task is to connect a content offer to product education or pipeline

Use `free-tool-strategy` for interactive tools rather than downloadable or
static lead magnets.

## Context Prerequisites

- Check `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` first if it exists.
- Clarify:
  - who the audience is
  - which buyer stage is targeted
  - current lead capture methods
  - available content or expertise to repurpose
  - the primary goal:
    - list growth
    - lead quality
    - product education
    - demand generation

## Discovery Questions

- What problem should the lead magnet solve?
- What existing assets could be repurposed?
- What format best matches the audience and stage?
- What resources exist for production and promotion?
- What happens after capture:
  - instant delivery
  - email delivery
  - nurture sequence
- What conversion or quality metrics matter most?

## Decision Framework

- Solve one specific problem well.
- Match the format to buyer stage and consumption friction.
- Prefer high perceived value with low time-to-value.
- Make the path from lead magnet to product naturally adjacent.
- Ask for the minimum information needed at capture.
- Treat distribution and follow-up as part of the offer design, not an
  afterthought.

## Output Structure

1. LEAD MAGNET RECOMMENDATION

State the format, topic, buyer stage, and why this is the right fit.

2. CONTENT OUTLINE

State the scope, key sections, and value promise.

3. GATING AND DELIVERY PLAN

State the capture model, form fields, delivery method, and thank-you-page next
step.

4. DISTRIBUTION PLAN

State the promotion channels, content upgrades, and amplification approach.

5. MEASUREMENT PLAN

State the KPIs, quality signals, and first test levers.

## Honesty And Non-Goals

- Do not recommend a broad, generic offer when the audience needs a specific
  problem solved.
- Do not over-gate weak content.
- Do not assume more form fields improve results without a quality rationale.
- Do not treat list growth as success if lead quality is poor.
- Do not use this skill to write the asset itself when the user needs finished
  copy or design output.
