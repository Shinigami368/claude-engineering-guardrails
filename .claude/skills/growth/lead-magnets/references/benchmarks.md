# Lead Magnet Measurement Benchmarks

Use this reference to frame lead magnet measurement without inventing generic
industry benchmarks that the repo cannot verify.

## What To Measure

- landing-page view to submission rate
- submission to asset-delivery success rate
- lead quality indicators:
  - relevant role or company fit
  - downstream reply, demo, or activation rate
- follow-up engagement:
  - thank-you-page CTA clicks
  - nurture open or click-through rates
- time-to-value signals:
  - downloads
  - template copies
  - worksheet completion

## Comparison Method

- Compare the lead magnet against the user's existing capture baseline.
- Segment by traffic source when intent varies materially.
- Treat lead quality as a first-class metric, not a footnote behind raw volume.
- Record both the offer and the follow-up sequence when evaluating outcomes.

## First Test Levers

- topic specificity
- title and value promise
- form length
- delivery method
- call-to-action copy
- landing-page context and proof

## Interpretation Rules

- A higher opt-in rate is not a win if downstream lead quality falls sharply.
- A lower-volume offer can still be correct if it produces better-fit pipeline.
- Do not claim universal benchmark targets unless the user provides their own
  validated comparison data.
