# Competitor Alternatives Page Templates

## Template 1: Singular Alternative Page
**Use when:** Comparing your product against ONE competitor
**Example:** "Notion vs. Coda" or "Our product vs. [Competitor]"

```markdown
# [Product Name] vs. [Competitor Name]

## The Short Answer
If you want [KEY DIFFERENTIATOR], choose [Your Product]. 
If you want [COMPETITOR STRENGTH], choose [Competitor].

## Key Differences

| Feature | [Your Product] | [Competitor] |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| Pricing | [Your Model] | [Their Model] |
| Best For | [Your Sweet Spot] | [Their Sweet Spot] |
| Setup Time | [Your Speed] | [Their Speed] |
| Integrations | [X connections] | [Y connections] |

## When to Choose [Your Product]
- ✅ [Use Case 1]
- ✅ [Use Case 2]
- ✅ [Use Case 3]

## When to Choose [Competitor]
- ⚠️ [Their Strength 1]
- ⚠️ [Their Strength 2]

## Real Customer Comparison
> "[Customer Quote about choosing YOUR product for THIS reason]"
> — [Name], [Role] at [Company]

## Try [Your Product] Free
[CTA: Start free trial →]
```

```markdown

We evaluated [X] tools based on: [Criteria 1], [Criteria 2], [Criteria 3].

```

## Template 3: You vs. Competitor Page

**Example:** "Coda vs. Notion — A Fair Comparison"

```markdown

we wanted to give you the facts so YOU can decide.

- **Their bias:** They obviously think they're better too

## Side-by-Side

|---|:---:|:---:|
| Founded | [Year] | [Year] |
| Pricing | [Model] | [Model] |
| Free Plan | [Details] | [Details] |

| [Key Feature 2] | [Details] | [Details] |
| [Key Feature 3] | [Details] | [Details] |

## Where [Product A] Wins
[Specific use cases with concrete reasons]

[CTA + Link]
```

## Quick Reference: Which Template to Use

| Your Goal | Template |
|-----------|----------|
| Position against ONE competitor | Template 1: Singular Alternative |
| Position against MANY competitors | Template 2: Plural Alternatives |
| Fair, balanced comparison (yours vs theirs) | Template 3: You vs. Competitor |
| Third-party comparison (neither is "yours") | Template 4: Competitor vs. Competitor |

## Conversion Elements (All Templates)

1. **Above the fold:** Clear recommendation + primary CTA
2. **Comparison table:** Scannable, honest, includes weaknesses
3. **Social proof:** Real customer quotes with specific outcomes
4. **FAQ section:** Address common objections
5. **Exit intent:** For readers leaning toward competitor
