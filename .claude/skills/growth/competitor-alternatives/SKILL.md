# Skill: competitor-alternatives

## When To Activate

Use this skill when:
- the user needs competitor comparison pages, alternative pages, or vs pages
- the task is to support SEO capture around competitor-intent searches
- the task is to support evaluation or sales positioning with honest comparison
  content
- the user needs a prioritized page-set plan for competitor content

Use `sales-enablement` for internal battle cards or objection handling that is
not public-facing page content.

## Context Prerequisites

- Check `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` first if it exists.
- Clarify:
  - your product positioning
  - the competitor set
  - the desired page format
  - the goal:
    - SEO traffic
    - evaluation conversion
    - sales enablement
- Gather or confirm real evidence for pricing, strengths, weaknesses, and
  migration claims before drafting comparison copy.

Use the bundled references for templates and centralized competitor-data
structure:
- [references/templates.md](references/templates.md)
- [references/content-architecture.md](references/content-architecture.md)

## Discovery Questions

- Which competitor terms matter most?
- What format is needed:
  - singular alternative
  - plural alternatives
  - you vs competitor
  - competitor vs competitor
- What are the honest reasons customers switch to or from the competitor?
- What evidence exists:
  - pricing
  - feature differences
  - testimonials
  - migration support
- Is the immediate need one page, a page set, or a reusable competitor data
  model?

## Decision Framework

- Optimize for trust, not smear content.
- Acknowledge competitor strengths and your own limits where relevant.
- Explain why differences matter instead of relying only on checklists.
- Match the page format to the search intent.
- Centralize competitor facts so public pages can be updated consistently.
- Prefer fewer evidence-backed comparison claims over broader but weak copy.

## Output Structure

1. PAGE STRATEGY

State the target page type, search intent, and priority.

2. COMPETITOR DATA FOUNDATION

Provide the reusable competitor facts, strengths, weaknesses, and migration
notes needed for the content.

3. PAGE OUTLINE OR COPY

Provide the page structure or draft content with sections matched to the page
format.

4. PROOF AND MAINTENANCE NEEDS

State what claims need verification, what assets are missing, and how the
competitor data should be maintained.

## Honesty And Non-Goals

- Do not invent competitor pricing, features, customer complaints, or search
  volume.
- Do not write deceptive or unfair comparison copy.
- Do not rely on a comparison table alone when the page needs explanatory
  context.
- Do not promise SEO results from the page format itself.
- Do not mix public comparison pages with internal-only sales collateral unless
  the user explicitly wants both.
