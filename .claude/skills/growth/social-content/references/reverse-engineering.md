# Reverse Engineering Competitors on Social

## Overview

Reverse engineering = systematically analyzing competitor content to understand what's working and why.

## What to Analyze

### Content Analysis
| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|-------------------|
| Topics | What themes resonate |
| Formats | What content types they use |
| Posting frequency | How often they post |
| Timing | When they post |
| Engagement | Likes, comments, shares |
| Growth | Follower trends over time |

### Engagement Patterns
| Metric | Good Signal |
|--------|-------------|
| High comments | Community, controversy, value |
| High shares | Viral, highly valuable |
| High saves | Evergreen, reference content |
| Consistent likes | Brand affinity, appeal |
| High views | Algorithm boost, strong hook |

## Analysis Frameworks

### The 80/20 Analysis
```
1. Identify top 20% of their posts (by engagement)
2. Categorize what made them successful
3. Create content calendar around themes

Typically:
- 20% of posts drive 80% of engagement
- Find those themes and double down
```

### The Gap Analysis
```
1. What topics are they NOT covering?
2. What questions do their comments ask?
3. What formats are they NOT using?
4. What audiences are they NOT addressing?

→ These gaps = your opportunities
```

### The Hook Analysis
```
For each top post, identify:

WHAT HOOK:
- Stat/Fact
- Question
- Bold claim
- Story
- Contrarian take
- How-to
- List

WHERE HOOK APPEARS:
- First line
- First few seconds (video)
- Thumbnail
- CTA

WHY IT WORKS:
- Pattern interrupt
- Relevance
- Specificity
- Emotion
```

## Step-by-Step Reverse Engineering

### Step 1: Identify Competitors
```
Primary competitors (same product/service)
├── Competitor A
├── Competitor B
└── Competitor C

Secondary competitors (same audience)
├── Influencer 1
├── Influencer 2
└── Brand in adjacent space
```

### Step 2: Gather Content (30 days minimum)
```
Create spreadsheet with columns:
- Date
- Platform
- Content Type
- Topic/Theme
- Hook Used
- Engagement (likes, comments, shares)
- Comments (sentiment, common questions)
- Notes
```

### Step 3: Categorize & Tag
```
Content Types:
- Educational
- Entertaining
- Promotional
- Community
- Behind-the-scenes
- User-generated
- Engagement bait

Topics (use consistent tags):
- Product features
- Industry news
- How-to/Tutorials
- Thought leadership
- Company culture
- Customer stories
```

### Step 4: Find Patterns
```
Look for:
✓ Most common topics
✓ Most common formats
✓ Optimal posting times
✓ Hook types that perform
✓ Content that fails
✓ Engagement tactics
✓ Calls to action used
```

### Step 5: Test & Validate
```
Take insights and:
1. Repurpose for your brand voice
2. Test similar content
3. Track results against baseline
4. Iterate based on data
```

## Competitor Audit Template

### Quick Audit (15 minutes per competitor)
```
1. What do they post about?
   - Top 3 topics

2. What format do they use most?
   - Video/Image/Text ratio

3. When do they post?

## Applying Insights

### Content Strategy Framework
```
INSIGHT: [What you found]

YOUR TAKE:
[How this applies to your brand]

EXECUTION:
[Specific content to create]

SUCCESS METRIC:
[How you'll measure]
```

```
INSIGHT: Competitor gets 3x more engagement on 
"day in the life" posts than product demos

YOUR TAKE: Audience values authenticity over polish

EXECUTION: Create weekly behind-the-scenes content

SUCCESS METRIC: 50% increase in engagement rate

## Common Mistakes

### Don't Copy, Adapt
```
❌ DON'T: Copy their exact content
✓ DO: Understand WHY it works and apply the PRINCIPLE

❌ DON'T: Copy their visual style exactly
✓ DO: Take the FORMAT and apply your BRAND

❌ DON'T: Steal their jokes/memes
✓ DO: Understand what CONTENT TYPES resonate
```

### Focus on Principles, Not Tactics
```
✓ Study: Why does this hook work?
✓ Study: What need does this content address?
✓ Study: What emotion does this trigger?

❌ Don't: Take screenshots and call it research
```
