## Why Use This Guide

Marketing copy often uses unnecessarily complex words. These alternatives make your copy more accessible, scannable, and human.

## Quick Rules

1. **Long word → Short word**: Most complex words have simple alternatives
2. **Jargon → Plain English**: Technical terms confuse readers
3. **Passive → Active**: "We did it" not "It was done"
4. **Nominalizations → Verbs**: "Make a decision" not "Make a decision"
5. **Hedge words → Direct**: "We believe" not "We tend to believe"

---

## Testing Your Copy

1. Read aloud - if it sounds stiff, simplify
2. Ask: "Would I say this to a friend?"
3. Use Hemingway App to check readability
4. Target: Grade 6-8 reading level for most audiences
